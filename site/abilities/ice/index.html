Best Aniimo for Ice

32 Aniimo and forms can work Ice in your Homeland: 3 at Lv 4, 9 at Lv 3, 11 at Lv 2, 9 at Lv 1. Prismana forms go up to Lv 4: Sugartini (Prismana Form), Glynsera (Prismana Form) and Jabster (Prismana Form).
