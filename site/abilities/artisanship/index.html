Best Aniimo for Artisanship

56 Aniimo and forms can work Artisanship in your Homeland: 12 at Lv 4, 25 at Lv 3, 4 at Lv 2, 15 at Lv 1. The best ones you can get without Prismana are Hoptail, Ignitis (Forest Form), Ignitis (Highland Form), Jabster and Jabster (Rainfall Form), all at Lv 3. Prismana forms go up to Lv 4: Fenmane (Prismana Form), Blazen (Prismana Form) and Ekrim (Prismana Form).
