Best Aniimo for Dark

50 Aniimo and forms can work Dark in your Homeland: 7 at Lv 4, 25 at Lv 3, 6 at Lv 2, 12 at Lv 1. The best ones you can get without Prismana are Fragrancier, Ignitis (Forest Form), Ignitis (Highland Form), Inferlupa and Pawney (Snowfield Form), all at Lv 3. Prismana forms go up to Lv 4: Ekrim (Prismana Form), Hexxin (Prismana Form) and Ignitis (Prismana Form).
