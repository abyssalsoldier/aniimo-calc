Questions

What does the Aniimo Homeland Calculator do?

Every Aniimo can work in your Homeland using abilities such as Fire, Water, Grass, Hauling, Artisanship, Leisure and Perfumery, each at level 1 to 4. Tell the calculator which abilities you need, the minimum level and how many workers, and it finds the smallest team that covers all of it. When two teams are the same size, the one with higher total levels wins. The search is exact, not a guess.

Why are Prismana forms left out by default?

Prismana forms are rare and hard to get, so the calculator plans without them unless you tick “Allow Prismana forms”. If a Prismana form would make your team smaller, or is the only way to meet a need, it tells you.

Can I plan with only the Aniimo I own?

Yes. Tick the Aniimo you own in the list, then turn on “Only use Aniimo I own”. Your owned list and settings are saved in your browser.

How up to date is the Aniimo list?

The list is checked against AniimoTools every day, so new Aniimo and forms appear shortly after they’re added to the game.
